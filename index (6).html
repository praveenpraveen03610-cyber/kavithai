Welcome to My Dear Reader ❤️

Your life is yours… but my heart belongs only to you. 🥺🖤 Live your life the way you want… I will never stand in the way of your happiness. I’ve always let you be free, like a butterfly in the sky. 🦋 If you’re angry, I’ll understand. If you’re hurting, I’ll be there. If you’re happy, I’ll make your happiness mine. My love is not about controlling you… It’s about understanding you and wanting nothing but your happiness. But… We truly love each other. Yet there’s a wall between our love— a wall that neither of us built. 🥺 I’m not ready to let you go… and you’re not ready to let go of your family. That’s why our love lives with one question every day— “Will we ever be together?” I will never control your life. Whatever you choose, even if it breaks me, I will respect your decision. But my heart has already chosen only you. One day, I’ll cross that wall and stand at your doorstep… If you’re still waiting for me, our love will become our life. And if you’re not… I’ll walk away silently, without ever blaming you. Just because we couldn’t be together doesn’t mean our love was a lie. Wherever you are, I only want you to be happy… But somewhere in my heart, I’ll keep one little hope alive— One day, the wall that separated us will lose to the love we shared. 🖤
